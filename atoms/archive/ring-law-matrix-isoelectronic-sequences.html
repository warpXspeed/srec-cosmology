Ring Law Matrix — four isoelectronic sequences

Each sequence holds its electron structure fixed and moves only the core charge Z. That isolates the ring variables. Three sequences fit a 3-parameter screening law σ = σ₀ + c/(Z−a); the fourth (He-like) puts two electrons in the same 1s ring and is treated separately as a pair-interaction measurement.
