H — framing status: what is measured and what is interpretation

Measured: screening constants by partner relation; a filled-ring residual; a half-filled exchange residual; a fixed-Z partner comparison; a two-parameter form that fits 6 of 7 sequences to the data floor.

Interpretation (NOT derived): that these are "ring" properties. The data require a collective explanation — the residuals appear only at special occupancies of a subshell and have no single-electron analogue — but no ring energies, resonance conditions, or quantum-number mapping were derived from the data. Calling them ring terms is a hypothesis consistent with the measurements, not a result of them. Standard physics calls the same effects exchange and correlation, which are also collective and also not single-particle properties.

The universality constant is the weakest claim in the set (see caveat 1): agreement between 1s and 2p inner partners may be an artifact of the effective-charge proxy rather than a law.

I — caveats and worklist

1. Proxy insensitivity. sigma = Z - Zeff is a linearized effective-charge accounting; its agreement across 1s and 2p partners may reflect insensitivity, not a law.
2. Residual ambiguity. The +0.164 held-out residual could be coherence OR proxy failure at complete subshells; only the spectral channel separates them.
3. Reproducibility is not correctness. Any replication shares the published values and inversion; NIST remains the authority for every number shown.
