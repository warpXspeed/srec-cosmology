Hold out the Ne-like sequence from all derivation. Report: σ₀ and a per sequence; a(1s|inner), a(2s|inner), a(2s|same-ring), a(2p|same-ring), a(2p|inner); the held-out Ne-like residual; and whether the s-partner or p-partner screens more at the same Z, measured without fitting.
