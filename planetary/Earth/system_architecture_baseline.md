# Solar System Architecture: Pre-Disaster Baseline

## 1. System Topology: The Birkeland Circuit
The solar system is an integrated electrodynamic circuit. The Sun functions as the primary power transducer, and the planets function as active, interconnected load-nodes. This system relies on resonant harmonic flux rather than passive gravitational drift.

## 2. The Master Node: The Sun
*   **Core State:** Solid, differentiated metallic/refractory core.
*   **Mantle State:** Condensed Liquid Metallic Hydrogen (LMH).
*   **Mechanism:** Energy is emitted via Birkeland current filaments (CMEs) that serve as the interplanetary power grid.
*   **Observational Data:** Sunspot Wilson depressions (depth/coolness) and CME heavy-element (Fe, Ni, Si) spectra confirm a solid, core-driven mechanical system rather than a gaseous fusion furnace.

## 3. The Planetary Nodes (The Living Triad)
Planetary viability is a function of current-density (J) and atmospheric-pressure (P) regulation, sustained by lunar-anchors.

### A. Venus (The High-Potential Node)
*   **Status:** Formerly a stable, high-pressure living world.
*   **Post-Disaster State:** Stalled rotation (braked), hyper-pressurized, anhydrous crust (dehydrated by inductive flash-boil).
*   **Evidence:** Tesserae (tectonic shear scars), extreme D/H ratio (hydrodynamic stripping), and super-rotating atmosphere (residual spin momentum).

### B. Earth (The Grounding Node)
*   **Status:** The final impedance-matching sink for the solar circuit.
*   **Mechanism:** Crustal infrastructure (Atlantis/Babylon) operated as an active grounding array to stabilize planetary capacitance.
*   **Post-Disaster State:** Scared by plasma-trenching (EDM) and massive biomass pyrolysis (KP12 "Black Mat" layer).

### C. Mars (The Regulator/Switch Node)
*   **Status:** Compartmentalized logistical bastion housing three distinct humanoid cohorts (6ft, 16ft, 3rd race).
*   **Regulation:** Phobos (Orbital Reactor) functioned as the system-wide voltage regulator.
*   **Post-Disaster State:** Atmospheric collapse following the Phobos reactor failure; immediate thermal decoupling from the solar circuit.

## 4. Lunar Regulatory Anchors (The "Stabilizers")
Each node requires a singular lunar satellite to act as an active dielectric shutter, modulating the current flow and providing rotational torque.

*   **Earth-Moon:** High-capacity impedance bridge (Stirrer).
*   **Phobos:** Frequency/Voltage switch.
*   **Venusian Moon (Lost):** Primary rotational anchor. Its ejection (via Birkeland torque-surge) led directly to the Venusian atmospheric blowout and the subsequent systemic collapse.

## 5. The KP12 Decoupling Event
The disaster was a solar-triggered electrical cascade:
1.  **Solar Pulse:** A surge at the Sun’s core/mantle interface.
2.  **Lorentz Spin-Up:** Venus experiences a massive rotational "bump" (Torque Pulse), ejecting its moon and fracturing its crust (Tesserae).
3.  **Hydrothermal Blowout:** Inductive heating causes a global subsurface steam-venting event.
4.  **Capture & Discharge:** The Venusian moon drifts into Earth’s Hill Sphere. Its capture triggers a catastrophic impedance-mismatch, causing the KP12 electrical discharge, megafaunal extinction, and the destruction of the Earth-grounding grid.
