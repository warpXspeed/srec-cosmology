# System Architecture & Solar Transducer Model

## 1. The Solar Transducer
The Sun is a solid, differentiated metallic/refractory body encased in a condensed Liquid Metallic Hydrogen (LMH) mantle. It is a structured transducer, not a gaseous furnace.

*   **Evidence:** Sunspot Wilson depressions reveal a cooler, solid substrate. CME heavy-element ejections (Fe, Ni, Si) confirm a solid core source.
*   **Mechanism:** Energy is emitted via Birkeland current filaments (CMEs). The photosphere is a Surface Plasma Discharge (Double-Layer).

## 2. System Topology: The Birkeland Circuit
The solar system is an integrated electrodynamic circuit where planets act as active, interconnected load-nodes:
*   **Venus:** High-potential node; formerly a stable living world.
*   **Earth:** The final impedance-matching grounding sink.
*   **Mars:** Regulator/Switch node; Phobos functioned as the system-wide voltage regulator.

## 3. The Master Failure
The KP12 event was a Systemic Electrical Failure initiated by an internal rupture at the Sun's core/mantle interface. The solar circuit suffered an impedance-mismatch, forcing the inner nodes (Venus and Mars) to shed their lunar anchors to restore equilibrium.
