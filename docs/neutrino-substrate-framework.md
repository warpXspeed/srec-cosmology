# The Neutrino Substrate Framework: A Structural Alternative to Dark Matter

## Status
Conceptual framework / working hypothesis. Not part of the Standard Model.

## Core Premise
The vacuum is not empty. It is a physical medium — a high-density sea of
uncoupled neutrino/antineutrino components. This substrate is the ground
state of reality.

## Key Postulates

1. **The Photon as a Composite**
   A photon is a bound neutrino–antineutrino pair (cf. de Broglie's neutrino
   theory of light). Its measured frequency is the rotational/alternation
   rate of the pair — a literal clock rate, not an abstract wave number.

2. **Charge as Rotation**
   Electric charge is not an intrinsic point-property. It is the net
   rotational torque of a toroidal field structure relative to the
   substrate. Charge sign = sense of rotation.

3. **Coupled vs. Uncoupled States**
   - *Coupled:* components locked into toroidal vortices → matter and light.
     Detectable electromagnetically.
   - *Uncoupled:* free components in the substrate → no EM signature.
     Detectable only through gravitational effects.

4. **Dark Matter Eliminated**
   No exotic WIMP is required. Galactic-scale gravitational anomalies are
   accounted for by the density of the uncoupled substrate itself. Note that
   dark matter is only invoked by the mainstream at galactic scales, never
   for solar or planetary systems — consistent with a diffuse medium model,
   not a particulate one.

5. **Baryon Asymmetry Resolved**
   Antimatter is not "missing." The matter/antimatter balance exists as the
   ratio between coupled matter and the uncoupled substrate. "Annihilation"
   is not destruction — it is de-coupling back into the substrate, with the
   released rotation propagating away as photons.

## Consequences
- Annihilation is a phase transition (re-pairing), not elimination.
- Wave peaks/valves are physical cross-sections of a rotating field.
- Mass is trapped rotation; energy is liberated rotation.
