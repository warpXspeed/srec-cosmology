## 2. Foundational Axioms (The Basic Premise)

1. **The Vacuum Substrate (Dark Aether):** 
   The vacuum is a physical medium composed of an isotropic, high-density sea of uncoupled neutrino ($\nu$) and antineutrino ($\bar{\nu}$) components in a ground-state equilibrium. 

2. **Uncoupled vs. Coupled States:**
   - **Uncoupled State:** Free $\nu/\bar{\nu}$ components in the substrate. They lack an electromagnetic coupling handle (zero net charge/spin-torque) and are electromagnetically invisible, interacting strictly via gravitational mass-energy equivalence and background pressure gradients.
   - **Coupled State:** Components locked into stable, rotating toroidal configurations (e.g., photons, electrons, protons) that exhibit observable electromagnetic and inertial characteristics.

3. **The Photon as a Composite State:**
   Following de Broglie's neutrino theory of light, a photon is defined as a bound, propagating $\nu-\bar{\nu}$ pair. Its frequency ($\nu$) corresponds directly to the internal rotational/alternation clock rate of the dual components, and its energy ($E = h\nu$) represents translational torque rather than abstract quantum packets.

4. **Charge as Geometric Rotation:**
   Electric charge is not an intrinsic, uncaused point-property. It is the net rotational kinetic state ("spin-torque") of a toroidal vortex relative to the local substrate. Sign ($\pm$) represents the directional sense of rotation.

5. **Annihilation as Phase Transition:**
   So-called "matter-antimatter annihilation" is not the destruction of matter into abstract energy, but a mechanical decoupling (decoherence) of high-torque toroidal structures back into the uncoupled $\nu/\bar{\nu}$ ground state, releasing their stored rotational energy as propagating photons.

1. **Galactic vs. Solar/Planetary Scales:**
